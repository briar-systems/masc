# the fixup kind each operand rule's symbol takes by default, by the relocation aaelf64 applies to its field
#
#   fixup <rule> | <kind>
#   fixup <instruction> <rule> | <kind>
#
# the kind is a constant of masc.isa.aarch64.fixup in lower case, and an instruction's line holds for it before its
# rule's. an add's and a load's or store's twelve bits take a symbol by a specifier alone, `:lo12:` and `:got_lo12:`,
# and a move wide's sixteen by its group's, so their kind is the one the specifier applies to. a field aaelf64 gives
# no relocation takes a local kind, which the builder writes only as the distance to a label of the section, as
# llvm-mc 23.1.1 resolves it and refuses any other target. the model states no relocation, so no line overrides it
