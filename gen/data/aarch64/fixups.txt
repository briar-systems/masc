#
#   fixup <rule> | <kind>
#   fixup <instruction> <rule> | <kind>
#
# the kind is a constant of masc.isa.aarch64.fixup in lower case, and an instruction's line holds for it before its
# rule's. an add's and a load's or store's twelve bits take a symbol by a specifier alone, `:lo12:` and `:got_lo12:`,
# so their kind is the one the specifier applies to. the model states no relocation, so no line overrides it
