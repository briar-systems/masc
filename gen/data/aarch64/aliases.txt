# the derivation of each alias of the instruction model whose operands are computed from its base's, or whose
# preference the model states by a function it does not spell, one line each:
#
#   derive <base instruction or row> <alias> | <derivation> [rank=<n>]
#
# the derivation is the pair of functions `<derivation>_base` and `<derivation>_alias` of masc.isa.aarch64.alias, and
# the rank orders the aliases of one row whose preferences overlap, the highest printed. a line of a row, by its key,
# as `dupm_z_i_.b`, derives the alias over that row alone, where the derivation reads a width no value states
