# the longer form of a branch whose target is out of range, and the code fill
#
#   relax <row> | to=<row> [flip=<mask> fixed=<member>,...] [args=<arg>,...]
#   far | row=<row>
#   fill | row=<row>
#
# a branch past its range becomes its inverse past the far branch to the target: cb<cc>'s nine bits reach 1KB, tbz's
# fourteen 32KB and cbz's and b.cond's nineteen 1MB, b's twenty-six 128MB. cb<cc> has no inverse of each condition in
# its own encoding, so its inverse is another condition over swapped registers, as cbgt x0, x1's is cbge x1, x0, or
# over the immediate one more or one less, as cbgt x0, #1's is cblt x0, #2 (A64 ISA XML cbcc_regs.xml and cbcc_imm.xml,
# and the aliases cble_regs.xml and cble_imm.xml), and an immediate past imm6's 0 to 63 leaves the row no longer form. a condition inverts by its low bit, but al and nv both mean
# always (DDI 0487 Table C1-1, "Standard condition codes"), so a branch on either has no inverse and no longer form.
# llvm-mc 23.1.1 and GNU as relax no aarch64 branch and refuse one out of range, so these are masc's own. the model
# states no relaxation, so no line overrides it
