#
# XED states no inverse. a jcc's condition is its opcode's low four bits, tttn, whose n bit negates the test, so each
# condition's inverse is the iclass of the opcode with that bit flipped, and every row of an iclass inverts to the row
# of the other whose target is as wide at the same widths and operand size. jcxz, jecxz, jrcxz and loop test a count no
# jump negates, so they have no inverse and no line
