# or that name no csr of the manual (#184)
#
#   csr <name> | rv32           a csr that exists only at rv32, which riscv-opcodes lists at every width
#   csr <name> | every width    a csr of every width, which riscv-opcodes lists at rv32 alone
#   csr <name> | skip=<reason>  a csr of riscv-opcodes the id space does not name, its number written as a number

# catalog does not hold
