# the hint spaces: encodings of a base instruction the manual reserves for
# hints, as terms over its bits in riscv-opcodes' own syntax
#
#   hint <file>::<name> | <terms>   a space of the base, every for one of no terms
#
# a term `hi..lo=v` or `bit=v` holds when the bits are `v`, `=a..b` when they
# lie from `a` to `b`, and `!=` negates either. an encoding is in the space
# when every term holds, so a constraint of the manual that reads "either x or
# y" is two spaces. a pseudo op whose fixed bits lie in a space of its base
# names a hint, and is a row of its own. the named hints in them are ntl.*,
# c.ntl.*, pause, the prefetches in ori's space and lpad in auipc's. the
# semihosting markers of slli and srai have no mnemonic and stay the base's.
# every encoding of the may-be-operations is one a later extension may
# redefine, so each is a space of no terms, and zicfiss's instructions in
# them, sspush, sspopchk, ssrdp, c.sspush and c.sspopchk (src/unpriv/cfi.adoc),
# are rows the way a named hint is. a pseudo op its base's own extension
# admits, as mop.r.5 of mop.r.N, is the base respelled and no hint.
# riscv-opcodes states no hint space, so no line overrides it
