# the enumerants whose mention in an operand needs none of the capabilities the grammar gives them, which the
# specification leaves open and spirv-val, the reference where it is silent, settles
#
#   mention <kind> <enumerant> | capabilities=none   an enumerant an operand names under its availability alone, its
#                                                    capabilities left to what uses the value it names
#
# SPIR-V 1.6 rev 7 3.2.20 BuiltIn, 3.2.30 Capability and 2.1 Language Capabilities give these builtins their
# capabilities and do not say whether naming one in a BuiltIn decoration is a use of it
mention BuiltIn PointSize | capabilities=none | SPIRV-Tools v2026.4, source/val/validate_instruction.cpp CheckRequiredCapabilities, and KhronosGroup/SPIRV-Tools#365 | capabilities=Shader
mention BuiltIn ClipDistance | capabilities=none | SPIRV-Tools v2026.4, source/val/validate_instruction.cpp CheckRequiredCapabilities, and KhronosGroup/SPIRV-Tools#365 | capabilities=ClipDistance
mention BuiltIn CullDistance | capabilities=none | SPIRV-Tools v2026.4, source/val/validate_instruction.cpp CheckRequiredCapabilities, and KhronosGroup/SPIRV-Tools#365 | capabilities=CullDistance
